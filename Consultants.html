QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Your questions about myIH.

Getting started

Who is myIH built for?

myIH is built for industrial hygiene teams and departments, including IH consultancies, CIHs, and in-house IH/EHS teams. It digitizes field notes and standardizes the sampling workflow, from planning to lab results.

Do you offer training?

Yes. Unlimited virtual training sessions.

What does our IT team need to do before we start?

Make sure your organization’s network allows access to the myIH web domain so your team can load the platform. The original implementation guidance recommends whitelisting the domain.

How do I find a project?

Use the Search Projects box in the Projects tab to filter by a project identifier. You can also adjust the number of rows shown per page.

Field notes and sampling

How does myIH standardize field documentation?

Prompted questions guide samplers through what to capture and keep their responses with the sampling information. The daily log records observations and events as the field day develops.

Can we tailor the questions to our projects?

Yes. Question groups can be assigned to projects so prompts focus on the work and observations relevant to the sampling assignment.

What types of prompted questions can we create?

Questions can use free text, dropdowns, multi-select, checkboxes, and radio options. Assign question groups to a project to guide the information samplers capture.

How does the sample plan support fieldwork?

Set up a sample plan in the Project Dashboard or import one using the myIH-format spreadsheet. The plan records who and what to sample and the planned sample count, helping the team follow the sampling assignment.

How are daily observations recorded?

The digital observation log keeps field notes together. Tags using @, #, and $ help associate and find information, while prompted responses capture the details your team expects for the assignment.

Does myIH record sampling equipment and calibration?

Yes. Record media, instrument and calibrator serial numbers linked to each sample, along with pre- and post-sampling calibration.

Can a CIH review the team’s field records remotely?

Yes. CIHs and managers can access the field notes and observations entered by the team to review context and follow up on details that need clarification.

Can we bring lab results into the survey?

Yes. Project Config includes an Upload Autoload Lab Results option for a user-initiated upload. Laboratory results can then be reviewed alongside the sampling records and field observations.

OELs and project records

How does myIH manage occupational exposure limits?

myIH supports OEL management, including designation of statutory and non-statutory limits. Dashboards display results as a percentage of the OEL and update as results are added.

What is included in an employee exposure note?

Employee exposure notes show the result against the OEL, plus the PPE worn and the date and time.

Does myIH create a sampling report?

No. myIH creates employee exposure notes, chain-of-custody documents, and survey-day PDFs and Excel files with all the context entered by the sampler. These records auto-generate and stay permanently on the project.

Can I download chain-of-custody documentation?

Yes. You can download chain-of-custody documentation from Project Config for a selected date range.

Exports and existing workflows

Can I export my records?

Yes. myIH offers one-click Excel and PDF exports, so your team can use the captured records in its existing review and documentation workflow.

Can we use myIH alongside Cority or another EHS system?

Yes. Export records from myIH, then import the files or place the PDFs in your other system’s document repository as supported by that system. This is a file-based workflow, not a native integration.

Can we use the data in BI tools or AI assistants?

Yes. Use Excel exports in your analytics workflow, or upload exported files to AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot. A shared SharePoint or Google Drive folder can hold exports for a BI workflow. These workflows use exported files.

Can you build export templates for our team?

Yes. Custom export templates can be built for your organization’s analytics and AI workflows.
